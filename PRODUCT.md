# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Families, shoppers, MSME owners, and sponsors in Kano and Northern Nigeria. They arrive to decide whether to attend or exhibit at GlitzFair — Kano’s first standard family funfair, launched by Bells Nigeria in April 2016. A separate site already sells tickets and takes vendor registration.

## Product Purpose

This site is the public face of GlitzFair’s 10th anniversary. It tells the decade story, shows the archive, and sends people out to buy tickets or register as vendors. Success is a visitor who understands the fair and leaves through the ticket or exhibit link.

## Brand Personality

Sunlit, communal, commemorative. Voice is plain and proud: business, family, fun. The interface should feel like walking into a packed Kano marquee, not like a SaaS events platform.

## Anti-references

Generic “premium events” templates. Fake Lagos summits and invented testimonials. Ticket checkout, vendor dashboards, and payment forms on this site. Soft-UI card grids, gradient headlines, and cream luxury palettes that could belong to any conference.

## Design Principles

- Show the real fair first. Photographs from the hall do more work than copy.
- One job per action: tickets and vendor signup leave this site.
- Ten years is the plot, not a badge.
- Keep names, places, and numbers that can be sourced. Do not invent editions.
- Design for families on a phone in bright light as much as for a desktop browser.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Visible focus, 4.5:1 body contrast, 44px touch targets, and reduced-motion alternatives. Copy and imagery should read as Northern Nigerian and family-inclusive.
